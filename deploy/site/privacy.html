Privacy Policy

Stoke · Effective October 25, 2026

Stoke is a dating and social app for adults (18+), currently in an invite-only beta. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and how to delete your data. "We" means the people who run Stoke. Questions: privacy@stoke.gay.

The short version: we collect what the app needs to work, we don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use third-party analytics or trackers. You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Sign-in. If you sign in with a passkey, we store only its public key. Face ID, Touch ID and your device passcode never leave your device. If you sign in with your phone, we store your phone number.
  • Your profiles. Everything you add to a profile: name, age, bio, photos, pronouns, height and other body details, what you're looking for, interests, kinks, position and relationship preferences. This can reveal sensitive information, such as your sexual preferences. It's shown to other members according to your settings.
  • Photos. When you upload a photo we strip its embedded metadata, such as GPS location and camera details, before storing it.
  • Location. If you allow it, your device's location, used to show members near you. We store your latest coordinates and a neighborhood name. Other members see your distance at the precision you choose; they see your exact location only if you share it with them.
  • Messages. The text and photos you send, plus reactions and edits. We store them on our servers so they can be delivered; they are not end-to-end encrypted. View-once photos are deleted from storage after they're viewed, and unsent messages are deleted.
  • Lifeline. If you start a lifeline, we store its timer and your last known location. If you don't check in, the contacts you chose receive that location and your live location for 24 hours. Ended lifelines are deleted after 30 days.
  • Verification. If you verify your profile, we compare a selfie with your photos. The selfie is not stored; we keep only the result.
  • Discover activity. Which profiles you open and message, used to rank what Discover shows you.
  • Safety. People you block, and reports you make or that are made about you (including any message that was reported).
  • Payments. If you subscribe, Stripe handles your payment. We keep a Stripe customer and subscription ID; we never see or store your card number.
  • Device and server data. A push notification token, if notifications are on, and server logs (IP address, time and the request made), kept for a short time for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

  • To run Stoke: show your profiles, find people near you, deliver messages and notifications.
  • To keep members safe: review reports, enforce blocks, detect explicit photos, and verify profiles.
  • To prevent abuse, such as limiting how often sign-in codes can be requested.
  • To process subscriptions.

We don't sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Who helps us run Stoke

These providers process data on our behalf, only to provide their service:

  • Amazon Web Services: hosts our servers and database in the United States. Amazon Rekognition analyzes uploaded photos to detect faces, describe content and flag explicit images, and compares verification selfies.
  • Cloudflare: stores photos and our database backups (R2).
  • Twilio: sends sign-in codes by text message, so it receives your phone number.
  • Stripe: processes subscription payments.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service: deliver notifications. Notifications contain a generic title such as "New message", never your message text.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim): turns your coordinates into a neighborhood name.
  • OpenFreeMap: provides map images. Your device requests them directly, so it sees your IP address and the map area you view.

Other members see what you put on your profiles, and the people you message see your messages. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. To delete it, go to Settings → Delete Account in the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account, profiles, groups you own, photos, and your conversations (including the other person's side of them) are removed.

Our nightly database backups are kept for 30 days, and server snapshots for 7 days, so deleted data is gone from every copy within 30 days. If you can't access the app, email privacy@stoke.gay from the phone number or details on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Your choices

  • View and edit your profile information in the app at any time.
  • Turn location and notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, at privacy@stoke.gay.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Photos are stored privately and loaded through links that expire. Passkeys mean there's no password of yours for us to leak. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Age

Stoke is only for people 18 and older. If we learn an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
